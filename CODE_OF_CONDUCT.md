# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors and maintainers must communicate respectfully, focus criticism on technical work, welcome constructive disagreement, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or disclosure of private operational data.

## Scope

This standard applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions, release channels, and public interactions representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's profile contact. Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior harms the project or its community.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, prior behavior, and the safety of affected participants. Reports are handled as confidentially as practical.
